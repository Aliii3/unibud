Five screens. Nothing to configure, no onboarding tour, no streaks to keep alive.

Subjects

A folder for every subject

Add the subjects you are taking this term. Everything else — documents, tasks, deadlines — lives in the folder it belongs to, rather than in one endless list you stop reading by week three. The home screen shows what each subject owes you at a glance.

Deadlines

Nothing arrives as a surprise

Add assignments, quizzes and exams with the date they are due. Unibud sorts them by what comes first, flags anything overdue, and reminds you a set number of hours ahead. Standalone reminders work too, for the things that belong to no subject at all.

Timetable

Your week, class by class

Put your timetable in once. Class times sit beside everything else you owe, so the week is one picture instead of three apps and a screenshot of a spreadsheet.

Tasks & check-in

One question a day

Tasks know which subject they belong to, and show up both on that subject and in one combined list. Then, at a time you choose, Unibud sends a single notification asking what is due — so an assignment set three weeks ago does not become tomorrow's problem.

Privacy

There is no cloud. There is just your phone.

Unibud has no sign-up, because there is nothing to sign up to. Every subject, deadline and file you add is written to a database on your device and stays there. It is not a promise about how carefully your data is handled somewhere else — there is no somewhere else.

  • AccountsNone. Nothing to create, nothing to lose.
  • Network requestsZero. The app never contacts a server.
  • Analytics & adsNeither, and no third-party SDKs.
  • OfflineEverything works with the plane mode on.

Questions worth answering first.

Does it sync between devices?

No. Your data lives on one device, which is the trade for having no account and no server. If that is a dealbreaker, this is the wrong app and better to know now.

What happens to my files?

Attach lecture notes and slides to a subject and Unibud keeps them filed there with a list of the chapters you are revising against each one. It stores and lists them; it does not open them for reading.

Will it nag me?

One check-in a day, at a time you pick, plus the deadline reminders you set yourself. There are no streaks, badges or motivational push notifications.

What does it cost?

Free, with no in-app purchases and no subscription. There is no paid tier holding features back.
